Delete your OnlyIdeas account

You can delete your account without the installed app. Open the web reading room, sign in to the account you want to delete, then open Profile → Delete account and confirm.

Deletion removes your active account, private papers and figures, notes, comments, agent conversations, conversion requests and app sessions. Private offline downloads are removed on the device performing deletion; other devices clear them when they next connect. Previously published GitHub copies, repository history, forks and copies saved by others may remain under their public license. Recovery backups expire within 30 days. Minimal security/revocation records may remain to prevent abuse or restoration of deleted data.

Apple-only accounts can delete directly in the iOS app. If you no longer have access, email contact@lazying.art with the subject “OnlyIdeas account deletion” and your display name. We will verify ownership before deleting data. Do not send your password or identity token.
