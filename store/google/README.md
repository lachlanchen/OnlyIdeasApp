# Google Play assets

`listing-en-US.json` holds the English store text. `icon.png` is the 512 px app
icon; `feature.png` is the 1024 × 500 feature graphic from `feature.svg`.
The icon and feature graphic were declared as AI-assisted during upload.

The feature graphic now reuses the approved flowing cyan/blue/violet icon, with
its gold dot and ivory background (8 October 2026). Regenerate it with
`node tools/native/export-play-feature.mjs`; the full icon exporter also runs
this step. Keep the banner and standalone listing icon on the same artwork.

The two 1080 × 2340 screenshots are unaltered captures of the signed native
Android app: its original CC0 reading sample and its agent welcome screen.
They contain no private imported research or personal conversation. Build 6
retains these screens and improves mathematical layout inside imported papers.

Store upload does not mean review approval. Consult the release records and
Play Console for the current track status.
