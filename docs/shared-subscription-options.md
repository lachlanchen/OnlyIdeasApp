# Shared LazyingArt accounts and future plans

Owner direction, 3 October 2026: record these ideas for later consideration and
continue the current app-specific subscription work. The shared account direction
includes EchoMind, AiMemo, OnlyIdeas and Bunko; preserve existing accounts, private
data, purchase ownership and verified account-linking rules.

Possible future models are a combined OnlyIdeas/Bunko cloud plan, a subscriber
discount in the other app, or a broader LazyingArt bundle. None is selected yet.
Unified login alone must not silently grant cross-app benefits or merge credits.

A simple experience would have one Profile entry showing app coverage, remaining
allowances, renewal dates and the store or payment provider managing each plan.
Check existing benefits before offering a new purchase. Decide whether quotas
are shared, how existing subscribers transition, and how refunds, cancellation,
restore and verified linking work across providers before implementation. Check
the applicable store rules when evaluating a concrete offer.

OnlyIdeas keeps its current US$2.99 / 14.99 / 29.99 monthly catalog. Bunko has
separately approved cloud-only plans at those price levels; its books,
dictionaries and offline reading remain part of the app purchase. This note
does not activate billing, discounts, pooled quotas or a cross-app entitlement.

The Bunko counterpart is `docs/shared-account.md`. EchoMind owns the central
identity contract; each app owns its adapter and app-specific billing evidence.
