Privacy

Effective 27 September 2026. OnlyIdeas is operated by LazyingArt LLC. Contact contact@lazying.art about privacy or your account.

Your account and library

We store your account identifier and display name, your private papers and figures, notes, comments, reading requests and agent conversations to provide the app. GitHub login supplies a numeric account ID, username and chosen display name; its provider token is discarded after sign-in. Apple login uses an app-specific identifier and an optional name. We do not need your email address. Sign-in sessions use protected cookies or native secure storage and expire after 90 days of inactivity. Signing out removes that device’s session and private offline downloads.

Conversion and AI requests

When you request PDF conversion, the PDF is sent to Mathpix. Temporary source PDFs are removed from our service after successful conversion; failed uploads remain with your request until account deletion or support removal. You choose Shared or Only me before importing. Shared is the default in current apps; the converted paper becomes public only after source permission and community review. Only me keeps it private. Older app imports without a visibility choice stay private. Mathpix processes submissions under its own privacy policy.

The paper agent processes your chat and recent conversation on our connected workstation. Searches are sent to research indexes such as OpenAlex or arXiv, and requested PDFs are downloaded from their source. When you explicitly request a reading guide or translation, the selected paper text is sent to our configured AI provider, currently DeepSeek. AI outputs are labeled and may be inaccurate. Do not upload documents you are not permitted to share with these processors. There is no background conversion or translation.

Public contributions

Private papers and discussions are visible only to their account. Public comments show your display name after moderation. Choosing Shared requests publication after source permission and community review. Only me keeps the document private. Personal notes and agent conversations remain private even when the paper is shared. Approved text and figures are copied to the public OnlyIdeas-papers GitHub repository. Public content can be copied by others; GitHub history, forks, downloaded copies and rights already granted under a public license may remain after you remove your account. Never publish private information.

Security and retention

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. We keep operational security logs and database backups for recovery. Account deletion removes active account data and revokes all app sessions. Backup copies expire within 30 days and are not used to restore deleted accounts. Minimal revocation identifiers and records needed to address abuse or legal obligations may be retained. We do not sell personal data, use advertising identifiers, track you across other companies’ apps or run behavioral advertising.

Your choices

Use Profile → Delete account to delete your account. The web deletion page provides the same option without the installed app and a support route for lost access. You can delete your comments, block readers, sign out, and remove offline downloads. Contact us to request access or correction. OnlyIdeas is not directed to children under 13.
