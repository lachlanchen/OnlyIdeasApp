Support

Need help with OnlyIdeas? Email contact@lazying.art. Include the app version, paper title or request ID, and what happened. Do not send passwords, tokens, or confidential paper files.

Read a paper

Use the Agent tab to find open research or give it a direct PDF link. Tap Convert & add to save flowing text with equations and figures. You can also upload your own PDF. Current limits are 20 MB and 30 pages per PDF, with a shared daily allowance. A publisher landing page is not a direct PDF.

Report content

Use Report beside a comment, or email us with the paper or comment and the reason. Moderators review public contributions before publication. Block a reader from the comment menu; manage blocked readers in Profile.

Account and privacy

Use Profile for sign-in, sign-out and account deletion. For lost access, use the account deletion page. See our Privacy Policy and Community Terms.
