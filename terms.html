Community Terms

OnlyIdeas is a research reading community operated by LazyingArt LLC. By posting or requesting public publication, you agree to these terms.

Discuss ideas with care

No harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual exploitation, obscene content, spam, impersonation, doxxing, or illegal content. Do not publish anyone’s private information. Be accurate about sources and distinguish evidence from speculation. Do not use the service to violate another person’s rights.

Respect authors

Only upload documents you are authorized to process. Open access does not automatically permit republication. Public sharing requires an appropriate license or documented author permission, source attribution, and verification that figures are included in that permission. Shared is the default import choice; choosing it requests publication after review. Choose Only me for a private document. Public papers are mirrored to GitHub and may be copied or forked; public licenses and third-party copies may survive deletion.

Community review

Public comments and paper submissions are held for review before publication. We may decline or remove material and suspend abusive accounts. Report a comment in its menu, block its author, or email contact@lazying.art with a paper title/link and the issue. Blocking hides contributions and prevents interaction in both directions. You can unblock readers from Profile.

AI and availability

Conversions and AI requests have page, file-size and daily limits. AI outputs may be inaccurate and are not professional advice. We may change availability to keep the service reliable. Your private uploads and conversations are not published by an agent without your explicit request. Our Privacy Policy explains processing and deletion.
