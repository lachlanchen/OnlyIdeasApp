## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one beautifully resolved redesigned app icon for OnlyIdeas, a quiet social research reading app where people explore papers and share ideas. The attached image is the existing icon, supplied as the redesign target and brand reference. Owner feedback: it feels ugly and stiff, wants something free, relaxed, smooth, graceful and inviting. Redesign the geometry substantially while retaining subtle continuity with its open O / idea mark and teal-blue-violet plus golden accent palette. Art direction: an airy, warm pale ivory full-bleed square background; centered, a single fluid, generously rounded, moderately broad ribbon-like open O gesture, like a loose effortless brush sweep. Smooth organic curves with an elegant gently tapering soft end, no sharp speech-bubble tail. The flowing mark transitions cleanly through teal, soft blue and restrained lilac; a small warm apricot-gold idea dot rests naturally at the opening, with comfortable separation. Suggest an idea taking flight and open conversation with very few shapes. Integrate the O/i identity abstractly; avoid literally drawing a conventional information-sign letter i. Plenty of quiet negative space, beautifully balanced, friendly and sophisticated, distinctive silhouette readable at app-icon size, soft fresh color with sufficient contrast. Mostly flat, crisp vector-like finish, only subtle smooth color gradation, no shiny 3D, no glass, no extruded plastic, no texture, no ornamental details, no sparkles, no book/lightbulb/brain/atom cliches, no text, no border, no mockup, no device, no surrounding presentation layout. Produce a single high-resolution square icon filling the canvas; do not round the square canvas corners or bake in platform shadows.
